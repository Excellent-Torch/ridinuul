Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Ridinuul, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are provided.

Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if:

  • The item received is damaged or defective
  • The item received is significantly different from the description
  • The wrong item was delivered
  • The item was not delivered within the specified timeframe

Refund Request Timeframe

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of receiving your order. Requests made after this period may not be eligible for a refund.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Contact us at contact@ridinuul.com with your order number
  • Provide photos of the item (if damaged or defective)
  • Explain the reason for your refund request

Refund Process

Once your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed within 7-14 business days
  • The refund will be credited to your original payment method
  • You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is processed

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refunds:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original tags
  • Sale or clearance items (unless defective)
  • Custom or personalized orders

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted in cases where items show signs of use or are not in their original condition.
