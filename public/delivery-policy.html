Delivery Policy

Last updated: January 2026

We are committed to delivering your orders promptly and safely. This policy outlines our delivery procedures and timeframes.

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver to all areas within Sri Lanka. International shipping is not available at this time.

Delivery Timeframes

Location Estimated Delivery Time
Colombo District 1-2 business days
Western Province 2-3 business days
Other Provinces 3-5 business days
Remote Areas 5-7 business days

Note: Delivery times are estimates and may vary during peak seasons or due to unforeseen circumstances.

Delivery Charges

  • Colombo District: LKR 350
  • Western Province: LKR 400
  • Other Provinces: LKR 450
  • Free delivery on orders over LKR 5,000

Order Processing

  • Orders are processed within 1-2 business days
  • Orders placed after 3:00 PM will be processed the next business day
  • Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

  • A confirmation email with tracking information
  • SMS updates on delivery status
  • Courier contact details for inquiries

Delivery Attempts

  • Our courier will attempt delivery up to 2 times
  • If delivery fails, the package will be held at the nearest courier branch for 5 days
  • Unclaimed packages will be returned to us, and re-delivery charges may apply
